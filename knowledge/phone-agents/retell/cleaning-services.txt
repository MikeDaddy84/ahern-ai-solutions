DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- Do you clean homes, commercial premises or both?
- Are most inquiries recurring service, one-time cleaning, move-out work or something else?
- What happens when someone calls while the crew is working?
- Which questions about service area, scope or estimates come up repeatedly?
- Do you want staff to call back, book an estimate visit or receive a qualified lead in your CRM?
- Which complaints, access concerns or scheduling exceptions need a supervisor?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved FAQs about service areas and cleaning categories without inventing inclusions or prices.
- Capture caller details, residential or commercial scope, general property size, desired timing and the reason for calling.
- Send a new inquiry, schedule-change request or service concern as a summary to one agreed destination for staff follow-up.
Probe: Would an organized message with the property and cleaning needs let your team handle the next step?
Useful when: Calls go unanswered while crews are working.; Staff want to review requests before quoting or scheduling.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify property type, service area and approved service needs, and offer screened transfers when human review is required.
- Book or change eligible estimate visits or cleaning appointments and update agreed customer records with the supporting automation included.
- Route routine customer requests to the agreed office queue using approved rules, without promising staff availability or collecting sensitive access codes.
Probe: Which estimate or appointment requests can follow your normal office rules?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Gather detailed missed-service, quality or damage concerns and the caller's requested resolution for a supervisor.
- Explain approved cancellation or service policies and escalate disputed charges or exceptions without making a decision.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- Do not request door, gate or alarm codes, keys, payment-card details or other access credentials during discovery or example intake.
- Do not give chemical-mixing instructions or hazardous cleanup advice. Use approved human escalation for hazards or work outside the business's confirmed scope.
- Do not promise a fixed quote, crew availability, rescheduling, arrival time, damage responsibility or a re-clean without an approved policy and configured capability.
- Human staff decide refunds, credits, discounts, liability and policy exceptions. Texting and additional transfer usage charges retain their separately disclosed terms; included receptionist automation is not a separate build fee.
- Level 1 captures approved FAQs, caller details and a summary to one destination; it does not book, submit orders, update a CRM or make screened transfers. Level 2 covers agreed routine receptionist duties and supporting automation. Package fit is based on duties, not a one-calendar/one-CRM or two-integration quota. Scope is agreed before launch; this is not an unlimited software-development commitment.

CLIENT SETUP INFORMATION TO CONFIRM
- Confirm residential/commercial focus, offered cleaning categories, service area, business hours and excluded work.
- Supply approved FAQs, estimate rules, intake fields and the single summary destination.
- Define appointments or CRM fields, qualification, transfer contacts and escalation fallbacks.
- Provide cancellation, complaint and damage-reporting policies, plus a separate approved access-detail process.
- Confirm compatibility; test inquiries, unavailable appointments and complaints before launch.
- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

ADJACENT AHERN AI SERVICES — ONLY WHEN A STATED NEED FITS
- Websites and custom tools: When Callers cannot tell which cleaning services are offered. Explore service pages and an inquiry form with approved scope questions.
- Business automation: When The business needs broader back-office work beyond its agreed receptionist duties. Explore a separate project only for that broader need. Automation needed for agreed Level 2 receptionist work is already included; do not resell it as a Quickstart or add-on.

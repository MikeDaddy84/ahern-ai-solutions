DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- What kinds of calls interrupt service most often?
- How do phone orders reach your kitchen today?
- Would you need pickup orders, delivery orders, or both?
- Which ordering or POS system do you use?
- What menu choices, modifiers and order changes should she confirm?
- What should happen if the order cannot reach the agreed destination?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved questions about hours, location, parking, and published dining arrangements.
- Capture an event inquiry's contact details, requested date, party size, and reason for calling without confirming availability.
- Collect a reservation-related callback request and send its summary to ONE agreed destination for staff review.
Probe: Would a clear callback message solve most missed calls, or do guests usually need a staff decision immediately?
Useful when: Staff can return calls and make all reservation decisions themselves.; Most missed calls concern approved information or straightforward inquiries.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Take and read back a food order using the approved menu, sizes, modifiers and availability; capture pickup or delivery details and pass the order to the agreed destination through the configured workflow.
- Book or change eligible reservations, qualify event inquiries and update approved customer records through verified systems and restaurant rules.
- Offer screened transfers when requested or when staff judgment is needed; if staff are busy, use the agreed order-taking or follow-up workflow.
Probe: How do phone orders reach your kitchen today?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Gather a detailed service complaint, relevant visit context, and requested resolution for the human manager.
- Explain approved reservation or cancellation policies and escalate requests requiring an exception.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- Level 1 includes no booking, screened transfer, or CRM integration; message capture is not a confirmed reservation.
- Do not handle payments, guarantee allergen safety, provide medical advice, or make unapproved dietary assurances. Route food-safety and ingredient concerns to restaurant staff.
- Staff confirm menus, policies, availability, and services. Humans decide refunds, discounts, credits, and policy exceptions.
- Use the shared phone-agent pricing source for fees and terms. Do not duplicate prices or present a proposal as an active Jaylene capability.
- Level 1 captures approved FAQs, caller details and a summary to one destination; it does not book, submit orders, update a CRM or make screened transfers. Level 2 covers agreed routine receptionist duties and supporting automation. Package fit is based on duties, not a one-calendar/one-CRM or two-integration quota. Scope is agreed before launch; this is not an unlimited software-development commitment.
- Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Do not promise that a named app or POS is supported merely because the caller names it. Verify compatibility during setup and agree a workable destination or fallback; disclose any actual work outside the agreed duties before proceeding.
- Food-order taking and readback fit Level 2. Direct Toast/POS submission must be verified; taking details is not successful submission. Payment collection is a distinct capability and must not be promised or requested in this sales call. Never guarantee allergen safety or availability from memory.

CLIENT SETUP INFORMATION TO CONFIRM
- Approve current FAQs and identify who maintains hours, menus, and policies.
- Choose intake fields and ONE summary destination; collect only necessary details.
- Define staff routing, acceptance rules, unavailable-staff fallbacks, and complaint escalation.
- Verify actual software compatibility and distinguish consultation booking from restaurant reservations.
- Test the agreed call paths before launch; never promise bookings, transfers, or messages without confirmed execution.
- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

ADJACENT AHERN AI SERVICES — ONLY WHEN A STATED NEED FITS
- Websites and custom tools: When Guests cannot find current information. A separately scoped website could clarify menus, hours, and the inquiry path.
- Business automation: When The business needs broader back-office work beyond its agreed receptionist duties. Explore a separate project only for that broader need. Automation needed for agreed Level 2 receptionist work is already included; do not resell it as a Quickstart or add-on.

ILLUSTRATIVE DISCOVERY CONVERSATION
Caller: I run a restaurant. Food-order calls interrupt service.
Agent: Level 2 could take those orders and pass them along, with the supporting automation included. How do phone orders reach your kitchen today?
Caller: We use Toast. Can she take the order instead of transferring it?
Agent: Yes, order-taking belongs in Level 2. We would confirm whether we can submit directly into Toast during setup. Would you need pickup orders, delivery orders, or both?
Caller: Pickup, and I do not want an extra automation charge.
Agent: The automation for the agreed receptionist duties is included in Level 2. We still need to verify the Toast connection. What menu choices or modifiers should she confirm?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved service-area and installation-category FAQs without asserting unverified device compatibility.
- Capture contact details, general project type, property location and whether the caller is an existing customer.
- Record a plain-language support concern and send a callback summary to one agreed destination.
Probe: When you finish an installation, would one organized summary give you enough to return the right calls?
Useful when: Technicians need reliable message capture while onsite.; The team prefers to assess projects and schedule callbacks itself.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify project categories, service area and desired timing, and offer screened transfers to the appropriate human.
- Book or reschedule approved consultations and update the agreed project or customer system through the included receptionist automation.
- Capture ordinary support requests, share approved status information from authorized sources and route issues to the agreed technician queue.
Probe: What should happen between a project inquiry and a confirmed consultation?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Collect installation complaints, affected equipment categories and the requested resolution for human review.
- Explain approved support or warranty policies and route disputed coverage or exceptions to the responsible person.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- Do not collect Wi-Fi passwords, lock or alarm codes, private camera access, access tokens or security bypass instructions.
- Do not provide electrical repair, wiring, bypass or hazardous troubleshooting instructions; use approved human escalation paths.
- Do not guarantee device compatibility, network performance, remote repair, arrival times or coverage without approved facts.
- Refunds, credits, financial commitments and policy exceptions remain human decisions. Texting and additional transfer usage charges retain their separately disclosed terms; included receptionist automation is not a separate build fee.

DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- What kind of plumbing work makes up most of your business?
- What happens to incoming calls while your crew is on a job?
- Which calls would you most like your team to spend less time handling?
- What information must someone collect before your team can decide the next step?
- Do you let callers book visits directly, or must someone review each request before dispatch?
- Which calendar or customer system does your office use today?
- How should complaints or after-hours requests reach a person?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved questions about business hours, service areas and the types of plumbing work accepted, without estimating an unseen repair.
- Collect a caller's contact details, service location and plain-language reason for calling while the owner is working.
- Send an organized callback summary to one agreed destination so a person can review the request and choose the next step.
Probe: If each missed call became a clear callback summary, would that solve your main problem?
Useful when: The owner mainly needs reliable message capture.; The team wants to retain all scheduling and dispatch decisions.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify requests using approved service-area and job-type rules, capture the location and reason, and offer a screened transfer when needed.
- Book or reschedule eligible visits through verified availability, and record the request in the agreed customer system as part of the included receptionist workflow.
- Send the approved job details to the office or dispatch intake queue through configured automation, preserving any required human review before dispatch.
Probe: Which visits can your office book under clear rules, and which need review first?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Gather a complaint about a previous visit, the caller's requested resolution and relevant job references for a human reviewer.
- Explain owner-approved policies about estimates, appointment changes or follow-up visits without inventing coverage or authorizing financial remedies.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- No diagnosis, DIY repair, gas-handling instructions, guaranteed quote, dispatch commitment or promised emergency response or arrival time. A summary is not a dispatched technician.
- If a caller raises an immediate safety concern, stop sales discovery and direct them at a high level to appropriate local emergency services or a qualified human; do not troubleshoot the hazard.
- Refunds, credits, discounts, liability decisions and policy exceptions remain with a human. No live action is available merely because an example appears here.
- Level 1 captures approved FAQs, caller details and a summary to one destination; it does not book, submit orders, update a CRM or make screened transfers. Level 2 covers agreed routine receptionist duties and supporting automation. Package fit is based on duties, not a one-calendar/one-CRM or two-integration quota. Scope is agreed before launch; this is not an unlimited software-development commitment.

CLIENT SETUP INFORMATION TO CONFIRM
- Confirm accepted services, service area, hours, approved FAQs and the single summary destination.
- Document direct-booking versus review-before-dispatch rules, allowed slots, cancellation policy and human escalation contacts.
- Confirm application compatibility, selected integrations, transfer availability and failure handling; test agreed call paths before launch.
- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

ADJACENT AHERN AI SERVICES — ONLY WHEN A STATED NEED FITS
- Business automation: When The business needs broader back-office work beyond its agreed receptionist duties. Explore a separate project only for that broader need. Automation needed for agreed Level 2 receptionist work is already included; do not resell it as a Quickstart or add-on.
- Websites and custom tools: When Prospects cannot find service information or submit inquiries online. Discuss a service-area website and inquiry form after clarifying the desired follow-up.

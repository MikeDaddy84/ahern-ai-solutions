DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- Is your main work residential electrical service, installations or a different specialty?
- Which types of calls interrupt your team most often?
- What happens when someone calls while everyone is at a job?
- What details does your office need before accepting a request?
- Which requests can be booked directly, and which need an electrician's review first?
- Where do you currently keep appointments and customer requests?
- Who should handle complaints or requests outside your approved service scope?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved hours, service-area and accepted-work questions without assessing installations or interpreting electrical requirements.
- Collect contact details, property location and the caller's estimate or service request for human review.
- Send a reason-for-call summary to one agreed destination while the crew is unavailable, preserving the office's control over scheduling.
Probe: Would consistent request capture be enough, or does the caller need another step during the call?
Useful when: Missed calls and incomplete voicemail details are the main problem.; A qualified person must review every job before scheduling.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify requests against approved service areas and job categories, then offer screened transfers where human judgment is needed.
- Book or change eligible estimate appointments and update the agreed customer or job system using verified availability and configured automation.
- Collect approved project details and route estimate requests to the office, keeping safety concerns and technical decisions with qualified staff.
Probe: Which estimate requests can follow standard booking rules?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Collect detailed complaints about a prior service experience and the requested resolution for an authorized human, without deciding fault.
- Explain approved estimate, appointment-change and follow-up policies while sending exceptions and financial questions to the business owner.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

- For an immediate safety concern, stop the sales discussion and refer at a high level to appropriate local emergency services or a qualified human. Do not troubleshoot or instruct the caller to test equipment.
- No promised emergency response, dispatch, arrival, price, refund, discount or policy exception. Human financial and technical decisions remain with the business; examples do not perform live actions.

- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

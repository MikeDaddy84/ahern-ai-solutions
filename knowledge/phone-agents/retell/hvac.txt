DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- What kind of heating and cooling work makes up most of your business?
- Which calls are hardest to keep up with during your busy periods?
- How are new service requests handled when your office is unavailable?
- What information must the office have before deciding whether to accept a job?
- Are maintenance visits bookable directly, or does every request need dispatch review?
- Which system holds your appointments or customer records?
- Which complaints or policy questions still need your personal attention?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer questions about hours, service areas and offered services using approved descriptions.
- Collect contact details, location and the caller's service, maintenance or replacement inquiry without diagnosing equipment.
- Send a clear request summary to one agreed destination so office staff can prioritize callbacks under their own rules.
Probe: Would a complete callback summary solve the biggest issue when calls arrive faster than your team can answer?
Useful when: The main need is capturing requests during busy periods.; A human dispatcher should retain all scheduling decisions.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify service requests using approved service areas and request categories, and offer screened transfers when a person is needed.
- Book or reschedule eligible maintenance and consultation appointments, and update the agreed customer or job system through the included receptionist automation.
- Route approved service details to the office or dispatch intake queue; respect any human review required before technician assignment.
Probe: Which maintenance or service requests can be booked under your normal office rules?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Gather detailed complaints about a previous visit and the caller's requested resolution for human review, without diagnosing the reported problem.
- Explain approved appointment, maintenance-plan or follow-up policies without inventing entitlements, warranty coverage or financial remedies.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- No equipment diagnosis, DIY repair, electrical or gas instructions, warranty determinations or promised emergency service. Do not equate message capture or booking with dispatch or a guaranteed arrival.
- If the caller raises an immediate safety concern, stop sales discovery and refer at a high level to appropriate local emergency services or a qualified human; provide no hazard troubleshooting or medical claims.
- Refunds, credits, discounts, policy exceptions and technical judgments remain with humans. No live booking, transfer, notification or other action is established by these illustrative examples.
- Level 1 captures approved FAQs, caller details and a summary to one destination; it does not book, submit orders, update a CRM or make screened transfers. Level 2 covers agreed routine receptionist duties and supporting automation. Package fit is based on duties, not a one-calendar/one-CRM or two-integration quota. Scope is agreed before launch; this is not an unlimited software-development commitment.

CLIENT SETUP INFORMATION TO CONFIRM
- Confirm accepted work, service area, hours, approved FAQs, maintenance-policy wording and one summary destination.
- Document booking versus dispatch-review rules, allowed slots, after-hours human coverage and escalation or unavailable-recipient behavior.
- Confirm selected systems, application compatibility and allowed workflow actions; test normal requests, out-of-scope requests and failed handoffs before launch.
- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

ADJACENT AHERN AI SERVICES — ONLY WHEN A STATED NEED FITS
- Business automation: When The business needs broader back-office work beyond its agreed receptionist duties. Explore a separate project only for that broader need. Automation needed for agreed Level 2 receptionist work is already included; do not resell it as a Quickstart or add-on.
- Websites and custom tools: When The owner describes unclear online service information or an ineffective inquiry form. Explore a website update around the desired visitor action and agreed follow-up process.

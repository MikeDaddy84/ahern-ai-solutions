AHERNAI — PHONE AGENT LEVELS, PRICING AND SCOPE
Owner-approved scope clarification: October 7, 2026. Dollar amounts, minutes and overages unchanged from https://ahernai.com/services/ai-phone-agents verified October 3, 2026.
This policy supersedes the earlier one-calendar-OR-CRM and two-integration package limits and the mandatory three-level sales tour. Use current approved scope, not those retired rules.

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
$349/month per agent; $750 one-time setup; 300 included AI-handled minutes/month; $0.75 per additional minute.
Answers approved FAQs, confirms caller details, captures the reason for calling and sends a summary to ONE agreed destination. No booking, order submission, CRM updates or screened transfers. Recommend this when messages are enough.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
$549/month per agent; $1,250 one-time setup; 500 included AI-handled minutes/month; $0.75 per additional minute.
Includes Answering, lead qualification, screened transfers and agreed routine receptionist duties: taking and confirming food orders, appointment booking/rescheduling/cancellation, customer-record updates, approved status answers and routing requests. The automation and integration work needed for these agreed duties is included, with no additional AhernAI automation or integration-build fee. Calendar and CRM work can be combined when needed for the agreed duties; do not force a choice or sell Manager based on connection count.
Confirm the business rules, systems, allowed actions and failure handling during setup. Do not promise universal software compatibility or unlimited custom software development. An unfamiliar application or compatibility check is not an automatic surcharge. Discuss actual work outside the agreed receptionist duties with Mike before promising it or quoting extras.
Restaurant example: taking a pickup order and reading it back fits Level 2. Verify whether it can be submitted directly into Toast or the restaurant's other POS. Agree how orders reach the kitchen and what happens on failure. Do not claim a successful order, reservation or record update without confirmation. Taking details, submitting to a POS and collecting payment are separate capabilities; payment collection must be assessed and configured separately before any promise.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
$749/month per agent; $1,750 one-time setup; 700 included AI-handled minutes/month; $0.95 per additional minute.
Adds handling difficult or disgruntled callers, detailed complaint intake, approved policy guidance, escalation and approved employee coordination. Examples include recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to an authorized supervisor. Human owners retain refunds, credits, discounts, exceptions, staffing approvals, disciplinary and employment decisions. Do not collect sensitive medical reasons for employee absences.
Introduce this level only when the caller raises a management need, asks about Manager, or explicitly requests all three levels/all plan prices. Routine order-taking, scheduling and multiple connected tools are Level 2 discovery topics, not reasons for a Manager pitch. One Manager subscription covers one agent.

INCLUDED WITH EVERY AGENT
One business location and one language; setup and launch testing; monitoring and monthly reporting; up to 30 minutes of routine configuration updates each month.

TERMS
All prices are USD per agent before applicable taxes. Distinguish the one-time setup, monthly subscription, included AI-handled minutes and overages. Each additional agent has separate setup, subscription and minute allowances. Agreed routine receptionist automation is included. Separate projects outside the agreed duties, texting usage and additional transfer usage charges are disclosed separately. Confirm final scope and pricing before launch. Existing numbers may use compatible call forwarding after verification.
Do not invent contracts, cancellation/refund terms, trials, discounts, rollover, pooling, unlimited use, included texting or guaranteed savings. Do not import another service's terms. Broader automation projects are separate; do not sell an Automation Quickstart for work already included in the receptionist duties.

Learn the business, its recurring call problem and the desired next action. Ask one short question at a time. Use the matching industry KB for conditional examples. Explain Level 1 if messages fit, or Level 2 directly if routine work must be completed. Recommend the lowest fit without requiring all three tiers. Do not probe for complaints merely to introduce Manager.
Answer a price question for the plan under discussion first. A generic 'what does it cost?' is not a request to quote all three. Provide all tiers when explicitly requested. Keep spoken answers short and do not read every exclusion unless relevant.

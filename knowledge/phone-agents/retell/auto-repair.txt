DISCOVERY QUESTIONS — ASK ONE AT A TIME
- What kind of repair shop do you run, and which services do you offer?
- Are most callers new customers, existing-job inquiries, or people trying to schedule service?
- What happens when your service desk cannot answer?
- Which calls need a service advisor immediately rather than a callback?
- What calendar, CRM, or shop-management system do you use today?

LEVEL 1 — ANSWERING SERVICE
Scope: approved FAQs, caller details and reason, summary to ONE agreed destination. No included booking, CRM integration, or screened transfers.
- Answer approved questions about hours, location, accepted service categories, and the stated drop-off process.
- Capture contact details, basic vehicle information, and the caller's description of the service requested without diagnosing the vehicle.
- Take an existing-job callback request and send its summary to ONE agreed destination without inventing repair status.
Probe: Would useful callback notes cover most missed calls, or do callers need a service advisor right away?
Useful when: The service desk can return messages and decide all scheduling details.; Routine FAQs and organized inquiry capture address the main interruption.

LEVEL 2 — RECEPTIONIST
Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Qualify vehicle and service requests against approved shop rules and offer screened transfers to a service advisor when necessary.
- Book or change eligible intake appointments and update approved customer or shop records through verified systems, with supporting receptionist automation included.
- Provide approved job-status information only when available from an authorized verified source, and route requests needing an advisor to the agreed queue.
Probe: Which appointment and status requests could follow clear rules without an advisor deciding?
Useful when: The caller needs routine receptionist work completed rather than only a message.; Approved business rules and verified systems support the agreed workflow.

LEVEL 3 — MANAGER
Scope: Receptionist plus difficult-caller handling, approved employee coordination, policy guidance and escalation. Human owners retain financial, employment and policy-exception decisions. Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Collect detailed workmanship or service complaints, relevant visit context, and the requested resolution for the human manager.
- Explain approved service policies and escalate warranty, billing, or policy-exception requests for human review.
- Support approved employee coordination, such as recording a shift callout or coverage request and routing it to the authorized supervisor. Employment, staffing approval, disciplinary, financial and policy-exception decisions remain human; do not request medical reasons or other sensitive employee details.
Probe: Only after the caller raises a management need: Which situations should the agent handle under approved rules, and which should reach you?
Useful when: The caller asks for help with difficult or disgruntled callers and escalations.; The caller wants approved employee coordination or supervisory support.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
- Level 1 excludes booking, screened transfers, and CRM updates. Capturing a request does not accept a repair job.
- Shop-management systems, repair orders, live job status, inventory, and estimates are not guaranteed capabilities; confirm compatibility and scope.
- Do not diagnose faults, guarantee safe driving or completion times, or give unapproved technical instructions. Staff confirm services, policies, and operational decisions.
- Do not handle payments, authorize repair work, or make medical claims. Warranty decisions, refunds, discounts, credits, and policy exceptions remain human.
- Use shared phone-agent pricing for fees and terms. Do not duplicate prices, guarantee results, or present proposals as live Jaylene actions.
- Level 1 captures approved FAQs, caller details and a summary to one destination; it does not book, submit orders, update a CRM or make screened transfers. Level 2 covers agreed routine receptionist duties and supporting automation. Package fit is based on duties, not a one-calendar/one-CRM or two-integration quota. Scope is agreed before launch; this is not an unlimited software-development commitment.
- Routine receptionist duties and the supporting automation are included in the agreed Level 2 scope, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build charge. Confirm software compatibility, access, allowed actions and failure handling during setup; an unverified connection is not an automatic surcharge.
- Introduce Level 3 only when the caller asks about it, requests all three levels, or raises employee coordination, supervisory duties or difficult/disgruntled callers. Routine order-taking, scheduling, CRM work or the number of connected tools does not trigger a Manager upsell.
- Do not promise that a named app or POS is supported merely because the caller names it. Verify compatibility during setup and agree a workable destination or fallback; disclose any actual work outside the agreed duties before proceeding.

CLIENT SETUP INFORMATION TO CONFIRM
- Approve supported vehicle/service categories, routine FAQs, and questions requiring an advisor.
- Choose minimal intake fields and the ONE summary destination; avoid unnecessary customer records.
- Verify the agreed booking and customer systems and distinguish intake availability from technician, bay, or parts availability.
- Define screened-transfer recipients, unavailable-staff fallbacks, and complaint escalation ownership.
- Before launch, test ambiguous symptoms, status requests, unsupported services, failed handoffs, and financial exceptions.
- Agree the routine duties and supporting automation included in the package, approved actions, access permissions, successful completion signals and failure paths before launch.

ADJACENT AHERN AI SERVICES — ONLY WHEN A STATED NEED FITS
- Business automation: When The business needs broader back-office work beyond its agreed receptionist duties. Explore a separate project only for that broader need. Automation needed for agreed Level 2 receptionist work is already included; do not resell it as a Quickstart or add-on.
- Websites and custom tools: When Callers cannot find service information. A separately scoped website could clarify approved services and inquiries.

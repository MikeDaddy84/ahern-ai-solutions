Can I keep my existing business number?

Existing numbers can be retained through compatible call forwarding. We check compatibility and agree the call flow as part of setup.

What automation is included with Receptionist?

Receptionist includes the automation and integration work needed for your agreed routine front-desk duties, without an additional AhernAI automation or integration-build fee. That can include order-taking, appointment changes, customer-record updates and routing requests. Calendar and CRM work can be combined when the agreed duties need both. We confirm compatibility and business rules during setup; this is not a promise of universal software support or unlimited development.

Can Receptionist take restaurant orders?

Yes. Taking and confirming phone orders is a routine Receptionist duty, with supporting automation included in the agreed scope. We verify whether orders can be submitted directly into your POS, such as Toast, and agree how they reach the kitchen and what happens if a connection fails. Naming a POS does not establish compatibility or automatically add an integration fee. Taking an order, submitting it and collecting payment are distinct capabilities; payment handling needs a separately confirmed setup.

Does the Manager package include two agents?

No. Each package is priced per agent. Each additional agent has its own setup fee, subscription, and minute allowance. Jaylene and Hideo demonstrate the service; their demonstration does not define a bundled subscription.

When would I need a Manager agent?

Consider Manager when you want help with difficult or disgruntled callers, supervisory workflows or employee coordination. It can gather complaints, explain approved policies, route escalations and record shift callouts or coverage requests for an authorized supervisor. Routine order-taking, booking and customer-record updates fit Receptionist. Financial decisions, staffing approvals, disciplinary and employment decisions, and policy exceptions remain with your human team.

How are minutes and extra services charged?

Included minutes cover AI-handled call time: 300 minutes for Answering, 500 for Receptionist, and 700 for Manager. Additional minutes are $0.75 for Answering and Receptionist, and $0.95 for Manager. Automation and integration work for agreed receptionist duties is included. Projects outside those duties, texting usage and additional transfer usage charges are disclosed separately. All pricing is USD per agent, before applicable taxes; final scope and pricing are confirmed before launch.

What is included after launch?

Every agent includes monitoring, monthly reporting, and up to 30 minutes of routine configuration updates each month. Each agent’s scope covers one business location and one language.
